# Licensing

ironwork for COBOL is published under **AGPL-3.0-or-later with the ironwork Runtime Exception**.
That is the licence in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) and [`RUNTIME-EXCEPTION.md`](RUNTIME-EXCEPTION.md), and
the one every published artifact carries. If it works for you, take it: nothing here asks you to
talk to us first.

## Your programs stay yours

The AGPL covers ironwork. The exception keeps it off your COBOL:

- A program you compile, check or run with ironwork is not covered by the AGPL because ironwork
  handled it, and neither is anything ironwork produces from it.
- A compiled program may carry ironwork's runtime library and still be distributed on any terms
  you choose.
- Running a program under ironwork where users reach it over a network does not oblige you to
  publish that program.

What the AGPL does cover is ironwork itself. If you modify ironwork and offer the modified version
to others, including over a network, the AGPL's obligations apply to your modifications.

## What is on offer

| | For | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Anyone | AGPL-3.0-or-later with the Runtime Exception. Free. No application, no conversation. |
| **Internal use** | An organisation running ironwork on its own programs, whose policy refuses AGPL | [PolyForm Internal Use 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/internal-use/1.0.0/), unmodified, for a fee |
| **Everything else** | Redistributing or embedding a modified ironwork, OEM, delivery inside a consultancy engagement | Negotiated commercial licence |

PolyForm Internal Use does not permit you to offer ironwork to third parties. A consultancy or
modernisation partner running ironwork inside client engagements needs the negotiated tier.

**A PolyForm grant is not a commercial contract.** PolyForm licences are as-is: no warranty, no
indemnity, no liability cap, no support commitment. If your vendor onboarding requires those, ask
for the negotiated tier directly.

**Neither alternative tier is open source.** The AGPL distribution is; a PolyForm grant is
source-available, and we will not describe it otherwise.
